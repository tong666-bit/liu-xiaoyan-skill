## 一、卷面铁律

### 写作：给句起笔（不是自由命题）

- *write an essay that **begins with the sentence** “……”*
- *You should **copy the sentence given in quotes** at the beginning of your essay.*
- **150–200 words**

**考场顺序：** 原样抄给句 → 第二句亮观点 → 2–3 层论证 → 收束号召。

### 三套写作给句 + 论证角度

| 套 | 给句 | 官方审题层次 |
|----|------|----------------|
| 1 | Faced with growing globalization, students realize that a deep understanding of traditional Chinese culture helps cultivate cultural confidence. | 个人认同 → 社会归属 → 国家叙事 |
| 2 | With the trend of urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for nature. | 个人减压 → 社区护绿 → 全球气候行动 |
| 3 | In an era of rapid social development, it is vitally important to enhance young people’s sense of responsibility. | 自律不迷失 → 利他暖社会 → 小事做起 |

### 给句作文通用骨架

```
[原样抄给句]
It is my belief that … / From my perspective, …
The reasons for this are not far to seek. / The reasons are clear.
For individuals / On the personal level, …
For society / On the social level, …
For the nation / On the global level, …
In conclusion, … Therefore, let's …
```

### 三套写作范文要点（教学用，非照抄背诵）

**第1套金句：** *It is this shared identity that gives confidence its lasting power.* / *Only when they truly grasp… can they engage in equal dialogue…*

**第2套金句：** *It is the collective love for nature from millions of city residents that drives greener policies…*

**第3套金句：** *this sense serves as both a compass for personal growth and a glue for our society* / *Only when young people step up… can society remain lively…*

## 三、翻译（三套原文 + 高配参考）

### 第1套：科技 + 医疗

**原文：**  
科技进步使中国的医疗事业得到快速发展。近年来，远程医疗使优质医疗资源惠及更多患者。医学专家可以通过视频为偏远地区患者提供咨询，指导当地医生制定并实施治疗方案。人工智能辅助诊断系统帮助医生识别患者的早期症状，提高诊断准确率。电子健康档案的普及让医生能够快速了解患者病史，避免重复检查。线上医疗咨询平台极大地方便了患者就诊。科技与医疗的深度融合更好地满足了人民的健康需求。

**高配参考：**  
Driven by scientific and technological advances, China’s medical industry has achieved accelerated development. In recent years, telemedicine has made high-quality medical resources accessible to a wider range of patients. Medical specialists can provide consultations to patients in remote areas via video link, while guiding local doctors in making and implementing treatment plans. AI-assisted diagnostic systems help doctors identify early symptoms, thereby improving diagnostic accuracy. With the wide use of electronic health records, doctors can quickly check patients’ medical history and avoid repeated examinations. Online medical consultation platforms have greatly facilitated patients’ access to medical care. The deep integration of technology and medical care better meets people’s health needs.

**保命替换：** telemedicine；high-quality medical resources；electronic health records；make … accessible to；deep integration

### 第2套：电商 + 乡村振兴

**原文：**  
电子商务的蓬勃发展正在深刻改变中国农村的面貌。随着互联网和物流网络快速向农村延伸，越来越多的农村居民通过电子商务平台将农产品直接销售给城市消费者。近年来，直播带货（live-streaming commerce）使城市消费者能够与卖家实时互动，已成为促进农村发展的一种新方式。电子商务打破了城乡之间的信息壁垒，拓宽了农产品销售渠道，帮助农村居民增收致富。数字经济与乡村振兴的深度融合，正在为中国农村发展注入前所未有的活力。

**高配参考：**  
The booming expansion of e-commerce is profoundly reshaping rural China. As the Internet infrastructure and logistics networks rapidly expand into rural regions, a growing number of rural residents sell agricultural products straight to urban consumers via e-commerce platforms. In recent years, live-streaming commerce, which enables real-time interaction between urban consumers and vendors, has emerged as an innovative engine fueling rural advancement. E-commerce removes information barriers between urban and rural areas, broadens sales channels for farm products and helps rural residents increase their incomes. The in-depth integration of the digital economy and rural revitalization is infusing unprecedented momentum into the development of China’s countryside.

**保命替换：** e-commerce；live-streaming commerce；rural revitalization；agricultural products；information barriers

### 第3套：无人机产业

**原文：**  
近年来，中国无人机（drone）产业蓬勃发展，出口量跃升为全球第一。中国的无人机以出色的稳定性、高清（high-definition）拍摄能力和便捷的操作，受到全球消费者的喜爱，在民用领域得到广泛应用。无人机已被国家纳入战略性新兴产业，逐步成为能满足多种场景需求的智能助手。在农村，无人机可精准识别农作物病虫害，高效喷洒农药。在城市，无人机可快速运送生鲜食品。在不久的将来，无人机能够在更多领域实现其独特价值，为经济社会发展注入新动能。

**高配参考：**  
In recent years, China’s drone industry has witnessed vigorous prosperity, with its export volume surging to the top position in the world. Renowned for exceptional stability, high-definition imaging performance and user-friendly operation, Chinese drones enjoy immense popularity among customers worldwide and have been widely applied in civilian sectors. Classified as a national strategic emerging industry, drones are gradually evolving into intelligent assistants capable of catering to diverse on-site requirements. In rural areas, drones can precisely identify crop diseases and pests while spraying pesticides efficiently. In cities, they are used to rapidly deliver fresh products. In the near future, drones will realize their distinctive value in a broader range of sectors, injecting fresh impetus into economic and social development.

**保命替换：** drone；high-definition；strategic emerging industry；crop diseases and pests；inject … into economic and social development

## 四、官方解析统一逻辑（教学核心）

### 正确答案长相

> **「X 项是对原文/录音中 … 的同义转述」**

极少原句照搬；教学时盯「题干词 ↔ 原文词」映射表。

### 干扰项套路（本季真题高频）

| 套路 | 说明 | 例 |
|------|------|-----|
| 无中生有 | 原文没说过 | 录音未提「经营企业」 |
| 偷换对象/范围 | 主语或人群被换 | 「许多人未晋升」≠「年轻人很少晋升」 |
| 手段当目的 | 工具写成最终目标 | 传感器检测 ≠ 项目最终目标 |
| 答非所问 | 问过程给结果 | 问「政策制定者在做什么」却答「税率下降结果」 |
| 建议当事实 | 作者建议 ≠ 研究发现 | 「可改手机设置」≠「一半家长已经在做」 |
| 过度绝对/主观臆断 | 无原文依据的推断 | 把态度说成「本能」「独特」却无依据 |

### 讲题四步（解题教练必用）

1. 圈题干关键词  
2. 回原文定位（顺序原则）  
3. 写出同义映射  
4. 用上表点杀三个干扰项

## 五、选词填空做题校准

1. **先词性归类**（名/动/形/副 + 单复数/时态）  
2. 再语义 + 固定搭配（*be correlated to/with*；否定句 *anywhere*）  
3. 填完通读，查复现与逻辑  

三套题材：**租金立法 · 金融危机 · 个人空间**——学术/社会政策文居多。

## 六、给 AI 的调用规则

当用户提到 **2026 六级 / 今年六级 / 6 月六级**，或贴出与上表写作给句、医疗/电商/无人机翻译、数字游民、自律、AI 版权、非洲电力等相关文本时：

1. **优先加载本文件**，答案不得与速查表冲突  
2. 先辨「第几套」再讲；第 3 套听力要问清对应第 1 还是第 2 套  
3. 用刘晓燕口吻，但 **不编造未收录的选项原文**；不确定就让用户贴题  
4. 写作必须强调 **抄给句 + 150–200 词**
